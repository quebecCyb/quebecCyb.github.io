# Motivation Letter — NDR

**Subject: Application for Journalist / Research Contributor Position — NDR**

---

Dear NDR Editorial Team,

My name is Andrii Maslo. I am a Ukrainian researcher and journalist currently based in Sofia, Bulgaria, where I am pursuing an MEng in Communications in Aerospace Engineering at Sofia University. I am writing to express my strong interest in contributing to NDR's investigative and reporting work.

As a Ukrainian, I have a direct and personal stake in the fight against disinformation. This is not an abstract professional interest — it is a matter for the broader democratic information space. That conviction has shaped the direction of my work since I began my academic and professional career.

Over the past two years, I have worked as an NLP Researcher at the GATE Institute (Sofia) within its Information Environment Research team, where my focus has been on machine-generated text detection and disinformation analysis. This work resulted in a peer-reviewed publication — *BuST: A Siamese Transformer Model for AI and Disinformation Text Detection in Bulgarian* — presented at RANLP 2025 and the ACL Workshop on Misuse of Language Models (OMMM 2025). I also had the opportunity to share advances in disinformation detection with NATO headquarters during the "Bulgaria 2025" exercise. In parallel, I developed a practical tool supporting journalists and fact-checkers in identifying AI-generated content in Bulgarian-language media.

On the journalism side, I have spent the past year as a part-time journalist at Burgas Media, where I covered local events, conducted interviews, built a source network, and produced photo and written reports under deadline pressure. I want to be transparent: my journalism experience to date has been local and relatively straightforward — attending events, capturing short interviews, documenting stories. I have not yet conducted large-scale investigative reporting. That is precisely why I am reaching out to NDR.

I believe my strongest contribution lies at the intersection of journalism and data: OSINT-driven research, open-data collection, entity and network analysis, and the analytical side of information integrity work. I am comfortable working with large datasets, identifying patterns, tracing information flows, and building tools that support editorial decision-making. These are skills I can deploy from day one.

What I am looking for — and what I believe NDR can offer — is the opportunity to work under experienced investigative journalists who can guide me toward more rigorous, high-impact reporting. I am mobile and prepared to travel to relevant locations. I can collect and surface information under editorial direction. I am motivated not by the title, but by the work itself and by what it means in the current geopolitical context.

I am currently available for part-time engagement, as I have at least one more year of studies remaining. Beyond that, I am fully open to your proposals and to adapting to the format that makes most sense for your team.

My German is at B1 level and improving; my English is strong (IELTS Academic 7.0). I am fluent in Ukrainian, Russian, and Bulgarian.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be glad to speak further about how I might contribute to NDR's work.

Sincerely,
**Andrii Maslo**
